# Mingly.ai Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 2026_

This is a placeholder privacy policy used during early development, including for LinkedIn OAuth app registration. It reflects our actual current data practices but has not been reviewed by a lawyer and should be replaced with a properly reviewed version before public launch.

## Who we are

Mingly.ai ("we," "us") operates a social platform that helps career-oriented professionals discover compatible people and activities.

## What we collect

**From LinkedIn, when you sign in:**
We use "Sign In with LinkedIn using OpenID Connect." When you authenticate, LinkedIn shares your name, email address, profile photo, and a unique account identifier with us. We do not scrape your LinkedIn profile or access your LinkedIn connections, posts, or messages.

**Directly from you, during onboarding and use of the app:**
Professional context (role, industry, career stage, school), location (neighborhood-level), interests, activities, lifestyle preferences, availability, languages, pet information, spending preferences, and any messages or feedback you provide within the app.

**Automatically:**
Basic usage data (pages viewed, actions taken, timestamps) used to operate and improve recommendations.

## How we use it

- To authenticate you and maintain your account
- To generate recommendations of compatible people and activities
- To operate core features (messaging, activity plans, Circle relationships)
- To improve our recommendation system based on real outcomes (e.g., whether a suggested meeting happened)
- To enforce safety and trust policies (blocking, reporting, moderation)

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your information for advertising targeting.

## What's public vs. private

You control which profile fields are visible to other users versus used only internally for matching (`visible_on_profile` vs. `usable_for_matching`). Fields marked private for matching are never shown on your public profile even if used to compute recommendations.

## Data sharing

We do not share your personal data with third parties for their own marketing purposes. We may share data with service providers who help us operate the platform (e.g., hosting infrastructure), under contractual confidentiality obligations.

## Data retention and deletion

You can request account deletion at any time. Upon deletion, we remove your profile and personal data from active systems, subject to reasonable retention for legal, safety, or fraud-prevention purposes.

## Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. Contact us to exercise these rights.

## Security

We use industry-standard practices to protect your data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), secure session handling, and server-side enforcement of privacy and access controls.

## Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact: **privacy@mingly.ai**
